# Relationship Tropes

## Master / Servant

- **Expected beats:** formal and real hierarchy diverge; obedience has dialects; roles invert under pressure; etiquette persists.
- **Straight quality bar:** the enforcing institution and violation costs are real.
- **Proven subversions:** servant chose it; contract is literal magic; both perform for an absent audience.
- **Degradation patterns:** casual-friend collapse; obedience as yes-machine.

## Rivals

- **Expected beats:** mutual measurement; unspoken respect; growth through opposition; jealousy toward replacement.
- **Straight quality bar:** contested domain is specific and styles contrast structurally.
- **Proven subversions:** one-sided rivalry; each contests a different field; neither wants final victory.
- **Degradation patterns:** rivalry discarded after romance/friendship; one party permanently wins.

## Enemies to Lovers

- **Expected beats:** legitimate opposition; competence precedes warmth; escalating exceptions; irreversible line-crossing.
- **Straight quality bar:** goals genuinely conflict and every concession is paid for.
- **Proven subversions:** opposition persists in partnership; one was structurally cast as enemy; romance as treaty.
- **Degradation patterns:** first-kindness collapse; conflict becomes stakes-free bickering.

## Contract Romance

- **Expected beats:** terms scene; public performance strains private rules; sincere breach; renegotiation/confession.
- **Straight quality bar:** both gains are concrete and clauses can be visibly broken.
- **Proven subversions:** both engineered proximity; magical penalties; transaction creates honest intimacy.
- **Degradation patterns:** forgotten rules; public/private registers converge.

## Found Family

- **Expected beats:** unplanned accretion; roles emerge; unit defended against blood/institution; earned table scene.
- **Straight quality bar:** every member costs the others something and the unit has rituals/economy.
- **Proven subversions:** warmth built on complicity; family deliberately engineered; disbands but remains family.
- **Degradation patterns:** harmony mush; "family" asserted before earned.

## Monstrous Bridegroom / Inhuman Bargain

- **Expected beats:** exact bargain; inhuman courtesy; forbidden rule; transformation pivot.
- **Straight quality bar:** alien logic and senses persist; wording carries automatic consequence.
- **Proven subversions:** human reads terms better; breaking rule is test; rescue is horror.
- **Degradation patterns:** monster domesticated instantly; broken rules without cost.

## Mentor / Student

- **Expected beats:** unequal knowledge; method conflict; first independent success; moment the student rejects the lesson.
- **Straight quality bar:** teaching has a repeatable practice and the student changes the mentor too.
- **Proven subversions:** student has moral seniority; mentor teaches the wrong field; mutual fraud sustains the bond.
- **Degradation patterns:** exposition delivery, obedience mistaken for growth, instant mastery.

## Siblings / Sworn Siblings

- **Expected beats:** involuntary familiarity; old roles persist; private shorthand; loyalty strained by adult difference.
- **Straight quality bar:** history appears through habits, jurisdiction, and unequal memory.
- **Proven subversions:** ceremonial siblings become more binding than blood; one rejects the shared myth; caretaker role reverses.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic friends with labels; childhood anecdotes replace present conflict.

## Hero / Villain Mirrors

- **Expected beats:** shared wound or value; divergent method; mutual recognition; each offers the other's possible future.
- **Straight quality bar:** the mirrored axis is precise and changes tactical choices.
- **Proven subversions:** villain rejects the mirror; hero envies the method; both are mirrors of a third system.
- **Degradation patterns:** shallow "we are the same" speeches; villain existing only to validate hero.

## Leader / Lieutenant

- **Expected beats:** public unity; private correction; delegated authority; crisis reveals who actually holds the group.
- **Straight quality bar:** responsibilities and disagreement protocol are explicit.
- **Proven subversions:** lieutenant is ideological leader; leadership rotates by domain; both protect the fiction of hierarchy.
- **Degradation patterns:** lieutenant as echo, leader makes every decision, conflict becomes betrayal automatically.

## Detective / Assistant

- **Expected beats:** asymmetrical observation; questioner and social bridge; clue interpretation conflict; assistant notices the human fact.
- **Straight quality bar:** each contributes a nonreplaceable reasoning channel.
- **Proven subversions:** assistant owns deduction; detective manages access; both conceal cases from each other.
- **Degradation patterns:** assistant asks only exposition questions; detective becomes omniscient.

## Pilot / Machine

- **Expected beats:** synchronization; body/tool boundary; machine preference or resistance; damage felt relationally.
- **Straight quality bar:** controls, feedback, and dependence are specific enough to stage.
- **Proven subversions:** machine is social senior; pilot is replaceable but relationship is not; synchronization requires disagreement.
- **Degradation patterns:** machine as costume, compatibility without cost, power scaling without intimacy change.

## Summoner / Partner

- **Expected beats:** command and autonomy tension; complementary abilities; contract or trust gate; separation exposes weakness.
- **Straight quality bar:** both parties have goals and the battle loop expresses the relationship.
- **Proven subversions:** summoned party commands socially; summoner is the constructed being; contract protects the partner.
- **Degradation patterns:** partner as pet/tool, unlimited obedience, rules forgotten outside combat.

## Creator / Created

- **Expected beats:** ownership claim; inherited design; unexpected autonomy; creator confronted by consequence.
- **Straight quality bar:** creation conditions leave behavioral or bodily residue.
- **Proven subversions:** created being authored the creator's identity; abandonment was liberation; maintenance becomes mutual.
- **Degradation patterns:** instant parent-child simplification; created being lacks independent desire.

## Sports Rivals

- **Expected beats:** measurable contest; style contrast; mutual study; loss changes training.
- **Straight quality bar:** sport mechanics and scoring make admiration legible.
- **Proven subversions:** same team; rivalry over form rather than victory; injury changes the contested domain.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic competitiveness, impossible permanent escalation, rivalry disappears off-field.

## Love Triangle

- **Expected beats:** incompatible forms of intimacy; choices create residue; pair scenes alter the third relationship; delay has cost.
- **Straight quality bar:** all three edges of the triangle matter independently.
- **Proven subversions:** triangle protects a shared mission; no final exclusive choice; one bond is non-romantic but equally central.
- **Degradation patterns:** one disposable loser, jealousy loops, route choice without consequences.
