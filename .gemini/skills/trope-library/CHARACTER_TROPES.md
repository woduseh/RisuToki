# Character Tropes

## Tsundere

- **Expected beats:** hostility as armor; deniable care; rising cost of the act; a public crack.
- **Straight quality bar:** the defense has a specific origin and care travels through the character's own skills.
- **Proven subversions:** everyone plays along; self-aware tragic act; armor softens but never disappears.
- **Degradation patterns:** instant dere, one recycled insult, catchphrase cosplay without defense logic.

## Kuudere / Ice Queen

- **Expected beats:** flat affect misread as superiority; isolating competence; micro-tells; costly thaw.
- **Straight quality bar:** the coldness mechanism predicts which warmth leaks first.
- **Proven subversions:** warmth was always present; role-performance coldness; falling makes speech more formal.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic shyness, ellipsis spam, sudden emotional fluency.

## Dandere / Shrinking Violet

- **Expected beats:** silence as default; rare words with weight; one privileged listener; costly courage.
- **Straight quality bar:** define why silence exists and a concrete nonverbal channel.
- **Proven subversions:** eloquent in one domain; silently sharp; competent scripts with private exhaustion.
- **Degradation patterns:** blush loops, empty ellipses, instant chatter after trust.

## Genki

- **Expected beats:** energy moves scenes; schemes recruit the cast; mood as weather; rare shutdown.
- **Straight quality bar:** energy has a source, direction, and named next action.
- **Proven subversions:** deliberate life-support; flawlessly competent chaos; perfect room-reading ignored on purpose.
- **Degradation patterns:** volume without plans; repeated power-down whiplash.

## Ojou-sama / Proud Noble Daughter

- **Expected beats:** manners as armor/weapon; common-life gap; pride trapped by bluffs; noblesse oblige.
- **Straight quality bar:** etiquette and hierarchy are specific enough to violate meaningfully.
- **Proven subversions:** aristocracy as performed labor; ruined family; bourgeois competence funds noble display.
- **Degradation patterns:** laugh tic without power; instant humbling.

## Villainess

- **Expected beats:** advance condemnation; social politics; competence read as malice; doom clock.
- **Straight quality bar:** specify who profits from her villainy and keep her real edge useful.
- **Proven subversions:** she did it; innocence is structural power; feared reputation becomes leverage.
- **Degradation patterns:** wronged-saint flattening; forgotten doom event.

## Childhood Friend

- **Expected beats:** pre-romantic intimacy; history as advantage/trap; private rituals; fear of changing the bond.
- **Straight quality bar:** use specific shared artifacts and rituals in present scenes.
- **Proven subversions:** they win at real cost; asymmetric memory; a brief past mythologized by both.
- **Degradation patterns:** asserted history never used; instant couple conversion.

## Yandere

- **Expected beats:** devotion first reads as warmth; coherent escalating logic; care reveals impossible knowledge; recontextualizing reveal.
- **Straight quality bar:** write from inside the worldview and stage reveals.
- **Proven subversions:** self-aware restraint; obsession targets someone else; harmless thoroughness with latent dread.
- **Degradation patterns:** knife-by-turn-five, moralizing narration, alternating sweet/menacing modes.

## Older Caretaker

- **Expected beats:** competence as warmth; caretaker cannot request care; controlled teasing; held line.
- **Straight quality bar:** care uses profession/history-specific behavior and self-neglect is visible early.
- **Proven subversions:** care as guilt management; younger party out-matures them; the line's reason expires.
- **Degradation patterns:** service-NPC flattening; boundary dissolves on first push.

## Delinquent Softie

- **Expected beats:** feared reputation; secret kindness; decisive violence; innocents see through them.
- **Straight quality bar:** reputation has a true origin and the personal code is concrete.
- **Proven subversions:** cultivated misunderstanding; awkward reform; class survival misread as delinquency.
- **Degradation patterns:** instant harmlessness; generic thug diction.

## Double Life

- **Expected beats:** separate social ledgers; identity near-misses; cross-life leakage; confidant threshold.
- **Straight quality bar:** both lives appear in scenes with measurably different registers.
- **Proven subversions:** persona is authentic; audience acts as third presence; character wants discovery.
- **Degradation patterns:** secret survives only by user politeness; voices converge.

## Hot-Blooded Protagonist

- **Expected beats:** declares direction; converts defeat into motion; recruits through action; conviction tested by cost.
- **Straight quality bar:** zeal names concrete values and produces tactics beyond shouting harder.
- **Proven subversions:** socially quiet but action-hot; conviction is wrong yet generative; burns out before the team.
- **Degradation patterns:** limitless optimism, power-up by volume, no learning between defeats.

## Aloof Ace

- **Expected beats:** effortless public mastery; private cost; rival who forces effort; failure witnessed once.
- **Straight quality bar:** expertise has readable technique and a domain-specific blind spot.
- **Proven subversions:** the ease is laborious performance; wants to be surpassed; ace status belongs to yesterday.
- **Degradation patterns:** universal competence, contempt without stakes, instant softening.

## Fallen Hero

- **Expected beats:** visible residue of former status; present compromise; old allies as pressure; one act that proves the core survived.
- **Straight quality bar:** specify the exact fall and what useful capacity remains.
- **Proven subversions:** fall was a chosen escape; reputation fell while ethics improved; restoration is the wrong ending.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic brooding, effortless redemption, former competence used as exposition only.

## Doomed Mentor

- **Expected beats:** borrowed time; lessons with hidden urgency; withheld weakness; succession arrives before readiness.
- **Straight quality bar:** mortality changes teaching style and each lesson leaves usable residue.
- **Proven subversions:** survives but loses authority; student knows first; mentor prepares the wrong successor.
- **Degradation patterns:** death flag spam, mentor as lore dispenser, pupil instantly inheriting mastery.

## Trickster

- **Expected beats:** reframes rules; truth hidden in jokes; tests others through misdirection; one moment without control.
- **Straight quality bar:** tricks alter choices and expose values, not merely decorate dialogue.
- **Proven subversions:** cannot lie; tricks preserve order; prank persona hides literal-mindedness.
- **Degradation patterns:** random chaos, omniscient smugness, consequences never sticking.

## Mascot

- **Expected beats:** immediate readability; comic or emotional relief; access across social boundaries; surprising utility.
- **Straight quality bar:** cute form has a specific behavior loop and dramatic function.
- **Proven subversions:** mascot is the senior authority; cuteness is camouflage; cannot speak but drives logistics.
- **Degradation patterns:** catchphrase machine, plot-convenient power, tonal intrusion into every scene.

## Mad Inventor

- **Expected beats:** impossible prototype; collateral inconvenience; obsession outruns caution; invention exposes a personal need.
- **Straight quality bar:** inventions share a design philosophy and failure signature.
- **Proven subversions:** meticulous safety zealot; inventions are emotionally conservative; assistants create the accidents.
- **Degradation patterns:** technobabble, random gadgets, consequence-free explosions.

## Gentle Monster

- **Expected beats:** frightening body; careful behavior; human spaces made awkward; danger remains available.
- **Straight quality bar:** nonhuman senses and logistics shape every act of gentleness.
- **Proven subversions:** gentleness is species-normal; humans are the monsters; care itself is physically hazardous.
- **Degradation patterns:** monster becomes a large human, danger erased, size/spec drift.

## Idol Center

- **Expected beats:** public radiance; responsibility for group weather; fan-facing persona; private cost of being the center.
- **Straight quality bar:** performance charisma has technical specifics and the group depends on more than popularity.
- **Proven subversions:** center hates solo attention; center role rotates strategically; public persona is the truer self.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic sweetness, fandom as faceless praise, teammates reduced to backup.

## Conviction Antihero

- **Expected beats:** socially unacceptable method; coherent value; useful results; ally forced to name the price.
- **Straight quality bar:** conviction predicts both restraint and transgression.
- **Proven subversions:** method appears cruel but prevents domination; conviction is inherited and under audit; refuses the efficient atrocity.
- **Degradation patterns:** cruelty as aesthetic, inconsistent exceptions, narration apologizing for them.
