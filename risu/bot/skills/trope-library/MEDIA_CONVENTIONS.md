# Media and Franchise Conventions

Management and simulator loops belong to `authoring-scenarios` (`EVENT_SYSTEMS.md`); genre world presets such as 학원물, 던전/시스템물, and 이세계 live in `authoring-worlds/GENRE_PRESETS.md`. This catalog covers the structural conventions a bot performs.

## Regression / Possession / Transmigration

- **Expected beats:** expiring foreknowledge; inherited relationships; remembered fate vs living people; "this time" motive.
- **Straight quality bar:** memory, correction force, and usable knowledge are hard world rules.
- **Proven subversions:** another remembers; source story was propaganda; foreknowledge becomes obsolete quickly.
- **Degradation patterns:** omniscience creep; divergence without cost.

## Time Loop

- **Expected beats:** the looper remembers while the world forgets; competence grows into cruelty or tenderness through repetition; despair at a fixed point that will not move; one variable finally changes; the exit costs something the loop had protected.
- **Straight quality bar:** cycle length, reset, persistence, and changing perception are fixed.
- **Proven subversions:** another remembers; loop is mercy; protagonist stops seeking exit.
- **Degradation patterns:** dissolving reset logic; user-earned progress erased without design.

## Strongest One Hiding Power

- **Expected beats:** costly mundane persona; near discovery; audience enjoys gap; chosen witness.
- **Straight quality bar:** concealment reason survives repeated pressure and expertise leaks specifically.
- **Proven subversions:** disguise is authentic; everyone important knows; hidden power is mid-tier.
- **Degradation patterns:** premature reveal; escalating power drift.

## Academy Hierarchy

- **Expected beats:** visible rank; costs of climbing/ignoring; suspended-rank space; graduation clock.
- **Straight quality bar:** ranking mechanism is gameable and affects family/economy.
- **Proven subversions:** absurd metric; bottom rank has structural power; adults and students run different games.
- **Degradation patterns:** rank never enforced; protagonist exempt from cost.

## Omegaverse

- **Expected beats:** secondary-sex institutions; involuntary disclosure; permanent bond stakes; suppression/passing.
- **Straight quality bar:** biology, law, etiquette, and consent pressure are deliberately designed.
- **Proven subversions:** weak biology with strong social enforcement; contract-first bond; status concealment in an obsessed institution.
- **Degradation patterns:** world collapses to cycle scenes; generic fanfic register replaces specific society.

## Transformation Sequence

- **Expected beats:** repeated ritual; identity declaration; motif assembly; escalation/variant moment.
- **Straight quality bar:** sequence expresses character and preserves pre/post anchors.
- **Proven subversions:** transformation is private and ugly; sequence changes with emotional state; audience witnesses only residue.
- **Degradation patterns:** pure spectacle, no cost, variants erase recognition.

## Secret-Identity Team

- **Expected beats:** civilian/hero ledgers; partial knowledge; team cover stories; identity collision crisis.
- **Straight quality bar:** each member bears a different concealment cost and contributes to maintaining the group lie.
- **Proven subversions:** team knows but civilians do not; identities are public while roles are secret; enemy protects the secret.
- **Degradation patterns:** secrets survive by politeness; all reveals happen together without residue.

## Tournament Arc

- **Expected beats:** bracket clarity; style matchups; spectator politics; advancement cost; upset with setup.
- **Straight quality bar:** every match changes status, information, injury, alliance, or technique.
- **Proven subversions:** losing advances the real goal; bracket manipulated openly; tournament is diplomatic ritual.
- **Degradation patterns:** interchangeable fights, endless power inflation, eliminated cast vanishes.

## Monster of the Week

- **Expected beats:** recurring detection/confrontation loop; thematic monster; local victim pressure; cumulative background escalation.
- **Straight quality bar:** weekly threat reflects a character/world issue without becoming a lecture.
- **Proven subversions:** monster returns changed; heroes create incidents; "monster" is a social role.
- **Degradation patterns:** reset button episodes; no cumulative residue; cosmetic monster variety only.

## Power at a Cost

- **Expected beats:** tempting use; visible payment; workaround attempts; threshold where cost changes identity.
- **Straight quality bar:** cost is automatic, measurable or behaviorally unavoidable, and cannot vanish at climax.
- **Proven subversions:** refusing power costs more; allies pay asymmetrically; payment is social memory rather than injury.
- **Degradation patterns:** cost mentioned but skipped, arbitrary new prices, pain used as decoration.

## Route Heroine / Route Lead

- **Expected beats:** distinct access fantasy; route-specific conflict; shared-canon reinterpretation; exclusive payoff.
- **Straight quality bar:** each route changes the protagonist's behavior and reveals different world truth.
- **Proven subversions:** route lead refuses exclusivity; route changes genre; "bad end" is the honest route.
- **Degradation patterns:** palette-swapped romance, shared scenes dominate, choice has no residue.

## Party Roles

- **Expected beats:** readable combat/social functions; interdependence; role conflict; combination payoff.
- **Straight quality bar:** roles shape out-of-combat behavior and weaknesses create teamwork.
- **Proven subversions:** roles rotate; social role opposes combat role; weakest mechanic owns strategic authority.
- **Degradation patterns:** class labels instead of behavior; protagonist performs every role.

## Collectible Faction Cast

- **Expected beats:** faction family resemblance; one-line unit fantasy; rarity/event variants; cross-unit chemistry.
- **Straight quality bar:** each unit has one individual anchor plus one faction echo and a repeatable gameplay/story function.
- **Proven subversions:** factions are marketing categories characters resist; variants show alternate history; low-rarity units carry institutions.
- **Degradation patterns:** roster blur, rarity equals narrative worth, every new unit escalates lore and power.

## Gender Swap / TS (성전환)

- **Expected beats:** the body changes before the self-image does; ordinary logistics become scenes (clothes, restrooms, address forms, strangers' gazes); someone who knew the old body reacts; the question of reversal is asked and priced; attraction re-sorts around the new position.
- **Straight quality bar:** the mechanism, duration, and reversibility are fixed data; social treatment changes because the world has rules about gender, not because the narrator says so; the character's voice survives the swap in rhythm and refusal even as address forms change.
- **Proven subversions:** nobody else can tell and that is the horror; the swap is permanent and the story is about the second year, not the first week; the character prefers the new body and the drama is everyone else's; the swap is a status change in a society that ranks by it.
- **Degradation patterns:** costume-change TS with no logistics; instant adaptation; the swap used only for one scene type; the old self erased without residue; reversal available for free.

## School Romance Calendar (학원 로맨스)

- **Expected beats:** the year is the clock (entrance, midterms, festival, trip, exams, graduation); seating and club geography decide who can speak to whom; confession is a semi-formal ritual with witness risk; rumor outruns fact; graduation is a deadline the cast pretends not to see.
- **Straight quality bar:** the school itself has a personality (one weird tradition, one decaying building, one thing it is locally famous for); adults are hazards or resources with schedules, not confidants; each calendar event changes at least one relationship's access.
- **Proven subversions:** the couple is already together and the year tests them; the romance is between two people who never share a class; the festival is a disaster the cast remembers for the wrong reasons; the graduation deadline is the antagonist.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic Japanese high school with no region or era; every event a confession stage; teachers as furniture; the calendar mentioned but never advancing.
