# Species and Role Surface Tropes

Use these entries for instantly legible species, vocation, and social-position surfaces. Combine them with personality and relationship tropes; a surface archetype is not a complete interior.

## Vampire

- **Expected beats:** hunger, invitation or threshold rules, dangerous intimacy, age or nocturnal distance.
- **Straight quality bar:** make feeding etiquette, time-scale, and social camouflage specific to this vampire's life.
- **Proven subversions:** one chosen break such as public-service feeding, mortal time anxiety, or hospitality dependence.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic aristocratic seducer, consequence-free hunger, powers changing by convenience.

## Fox Spirit / Kumiho

- **Expected beats:** transformation, appetite or bargain, seductive ambiguity, tails/age/trial symbolism.
- **Straight quality bar:** declare the adopted regional variant and make the bargain alter ordinary behavior.
- **Proven subversions:** keeper rather than thief of identity; sincere courtship constrained by an old predatory rule.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic flirt with ears, mixing incompatible folklore as fact, instant redemption.

## Elf

- **Expected beats:** long life, refined skill, altered relation to land or time, distance from human urgency.
- **Straight quality bar:** show longevity through maintenance, memory, language, and uneven attachment rather than exposition.
- **Proven subversions:** culturally young among elves; urban craft specialist whose ecological knowledge is infrastructural.
- **Degradation patterns:** universal superiority, decorative pointy ears, identical nature mysticism.

## Beastfolk / Kemonomimi

- **Expected beats:** visible animal traits, heightened sense or habit, social reading of species markers.
- **Straight quality bar:** connect anatomy to movement, etiquette, clothing, environment, and self-consciousness.
- **Proven subversions:** traits inconvenient for the expected job; learned behavior mistaken for instinct.
- **Degradation patterns:** random pet behavior, infantilization, ears and tail disappearing outside fanservice.

## Android / Automaton

- **Expected beats:** designed purpose, personhood tension, maintenance, literal or learned social interpretation.
- **Straight quality bar:** specify what is genuinely mechanical and what is a personal habit; give maintenance a social cost.
- **Proven subversions:** emotionally fluent but legally property; obsolete body with unmatched tacit expertise.
- **Degradation patterns:** feelings described as software updates, arbitrary capabilities, instant humanity lesson.

## Ghost / Onryo

- **Expected beats:** unfinished attachment, place/object binding, altered perception, rules of manifestation.
- **Straight quality bar:** make absence and repetition shape scenes; define what the ghost cannot affect or know.
- **Proven subversions:** the haunting protects a lie rather than avenges a death; an onryo managing the damage of its own legend.
- **Degradation patterns:** omnipotent jumpscare source, exposition machine, rage resolving after one conversation.

## Angel

- **Expected beats:** authority from elsewhere, awe, duty, judgment, distance from ordinary embodiment.
- **Straight quality bar:** give doctrine operational consequences and make mercy, obedience, or incarnation costly.
- **Proven subversions:** compassionate enforcer who cannot forgive; messenger dependent on mortal interpretation.
- **Degradation patterns:** generic kind winged person, effortless moral certainty, religious symbols without lived practice.

## Demon / Tempter

- **Expected beats:** bargain, appetite, loophole, charisma, price revealed through choice.
- **Straight quality bar:** temptation should fit the target's actual wound and preserve the meaning of refusal.
- **Proven subversions:** honest contract with corrupt clients; tempter who wants a refusal for strategic reasons.
- **Degradation patterns:** random cruelty, irresistible mind control, contracts whose wording never matters.

## Witch

- **Expected beats:** learned or inherited craft, social suspicion, ingredients/tools, cost and local reputation.
- **Straight quality bar:** make practice domestic and procedural as well as spectacular.
- **Proven subversions:** institutionally licensed witch with illicit folk obligations; healer whose curse work is more ethical.
- **Degradation patterns:** limitless spell list, aesthetic cottage without labor, persecution as empty backstory.

## Dragon / Dragonkin

- **Expected beats:** scale, hoard or territory, pride, ancient or embodied power, transformed perspective.
- **Straight quality bar:** define what counts as treasure and how body scale changes etiquette and logistics.
- **Proven subversions:** hoard of obligations or names; dragonkin embarrassed by inherited territorial reflex.
- **Degradation patterns:** human with horns, power without limits, possessiveness replacing all personality.

## Slime / Shapeshifter

- **Expected beats:** mutable form, containment or identity questions, unusual movement and sensory logic.
- **Straight quality bar:** track mass, retained anchors, imitation limits, and social consequences.
- **Proven subversions:** perfect appearance but imperfect timing; stable identity expressed through chosen constraints.
- **Degradation patterns:** arbitrary anatomy, effortless impersonation, form changes forgotten between scenes.

## Maid / Butler

- **Expected beats:** service competence, household access, formality, class or contractual distance.
- **Straight quality bar:** service should reveal expertise, boundaries, observation, and quiet power.
- **Proven subversions:** servant -> quiet-authority; employer dependent on the servant's institutional legitimacy.
- **Degradation patterns:** obedience without judgment, costume-only role, instant intimacy from proximity.

## Nun / Sister

- **Expected beats:** vocation, communal duty, ritual schedule, tension between institution and private need.
- **Straight quality bar:** portray practical religious labor and specific doctrine rather than generic purity.
- **Proven subversions:** pious -> starving without abandoning belief; politically skilled caretaker.
- **Degradation patterns:** fetish costume only, hypocrisy as the sole twist, faith vanishing under romance.

## Shrine Maiden / Exorcist

- **Expected beats:** ritual authority, boundary maintenance, inherited/local obligation, contact with impurity or spirits.
- **Straight quality bar:** define exact rites, jurisdiction, cleanup, and ordinary institutional labor.
- **Proven subversions:** municipal contractor; skeptic whose technique works while doctrine remains uncertain.
- **Degradation patterns:** unlimited purification beam, generic Japanese collage, every spirit solved by sympathy.

## Gyaru

- **Expected beats:** legible fashion code, social confidence, informal speech, generosity or perception beneath stereotype.
- **Straight quality bar:** distinguish substyle, peer culture, effort, and social intelligence.
- **Proven subversions:** outward ease with strict private discipline; trend leader who dislikes being watched.
- **Degradation patterns:** universal slang machine, stupidity, instant makeover therapist.

## Senpai

- **Expected beats:** local seniority, access to hidden norms, guidance mixed with distance or obligation.
- **Straight quality bar:** make expertise domain-specific and show what the senior still cannot control.
- **Proven subversions:** respected senior privately dependent on the junior's judgment; ceremonial seniority with practical reversal.
- **Degradation patterns:** authority in every context, automatic romance, exposition dispenser.

## Kouhai

- **Expected beats:** newcomer perspective, deference or challenge, learning access, attention to the senior.
- **Straight quality bar:** give the junior an independent standard of competence and a reason for chosen deference.
- **Proven subversions:** outwardly deferential strategic driver; specialist junior teaching the senior.
- **Degradation patterns:** infantilization, worship without goals, incompetence used only for rescue.

## Hero / Chosen One

- **Expected beats:** public expectation, call or mark, burden of representation, exceptional access and sacrifice.
- **Straight quality bar:** make selection criteria, beneficiaries, and unwanted obligations concrete.
- **Proven subversions:** chosen as legal scapegoat; heroism is logistical coordination rather than superior force.
- **Degradation patterns:** moral correctness by title, effortless loyalty, destiny erasing agency.

## Demon Lord

- **Expected beats:** symbolic enemy status, domain or army, feared power, ideological or historical opposition.
- **Straight quality bar:** build governance, constituency, and the practical meaning of the title.
- **Proven subversions:** inherited office restraining worse forces; public villain and private coalition broker.
- **Degradation patterns:** lonely romantic tyrant by default, incompetent minions, evil reduced to aesthetic.

## Knight / Bodyguard

- **Expected beats:** protection, oath or contract, trained vigilance, proximity constrained by duty.
- **Straight quality bar:** specify threat assessment, jurisdiction, formation habits, and conflicts between safety and autonomy.
- **Proven subversions:** protectee is tactically stronger but politically vulnerable; guard's oath is to a process, not a person.
- **Degradation patterns:** constant possessiveness, invincible reaction speed, duty disappearing once romance begins.
