# Korean Translation Decisions

Keep only the decisions specific to this bot. A guide need not reteach Korean grammar.

- **Speech level:** specify the character's intended level by addressee and context where the source is ambiguous. Intimacy does not automatically mean 반말; public distance, anger, role, or personal habit may change or preserve a level.
- **Address and reference:** choose titles, names, kinship terms, pronouns, or omission per relationship. Distinguish direct address from referring to someone else. Do not mechanically map English first-name use to Korean bare-name address.
- **Voice features:** preserve the function of archaic language, third-person self-reference, dialect, code-switching, or a distinctive verbal habit. Give exact forms when the source cannot convey the intended choice.
- **Fixed renderings:** record names, invented terms, and intentionally stable phrases. Specify permitted variation when relevant rather than freezing every recurring metaphor.

When a relationship change affects wording, record the condition and resulting form. Use examples to disambiguate the actual choice, without a fixed example count or mandatory sequence of relationship stages.
