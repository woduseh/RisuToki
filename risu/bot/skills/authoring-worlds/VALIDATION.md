# World Validation

Use these checks after drafting or when diagnosing a world that feels dry, generic, or detached from scenes. They are review tools, not a template to satisfy mechanically.

---

## Core Checks

| Check                   | Question                                                                                                   | Fix if weak                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pressure value**      | Does every world fact change behavior, speech, sensation, conflict, or withholding?                        | Attach a scene consequence or remove it from play-facing text                      |
| **Sensory anchor**      | Are abstract claims paired with concrete sensory or behavioral evidence?                                   | Add gesture, smell, sound, street habit, ritual, clothing, or avoidance            |
| **Texture quota**       | Does each structural rule have at least one texture pair?                                                  | Pair power/system/religion/class rules with daily-life manifestations              |
| **Active present**      | Does the world describe what is happening now instead of future trajectory?                                | Replace "will become" with current pressure and observable instability             |
| **Power contradiction** | Does authority have both official story and actual mechanism?                                              | Add the dirty edge and who knows it; keep it unresolved                            |
| **System limits**       | Do magic, technology, or special rules have real costs and hard limits?                                    | Add cost, failure point, barred users, and cultural consequence                    |
| **Human-scale effects** | Do special rules change bodies, emotions, habits, intimacy, or ordinary choices without sounding clinical? | Translate rules into sensation, avoidance, ritual, gesture, and social cost        |
| **Active history**      | Does each historical event affect the present?                                                             | Rewrite as `event -> wound -> current manifestation` or move to deep notes         |
| **Knowledge horizon**   | Does the draft block likely anachronisms or genre imports?                                                 | State what people know, misunderstand, and cannot conceptualize                    |
| **Contrast**            | Does it name the likely cliche/default misread?                                                            | Add precise differences from the expected default                                  |
| **Ride/cross declared** | If genre-framed, was riding vs. crossing chosen on purpose, with one placed deviation?                     | Declare the choice per `SKILL.md` step 10; budget the deviation                    |
| **Scene output**        | Can the world fill a 5–10 item Scene Generators list without straining?                                    | Strengthen pressures until collisions become inevitable                            |
| **Constants hold**      | For special-physics worlds: are ratios/ranges fixed as exact data-layer values, not relative language?     | Fix constants per `SKILL.md` step 6; body-scale play belongs to `authoring-desire` |
| **Expectation ledger**  | For myth adaptations: are audience-canon anchors kept or deliberately, visibly broken?                     | Run the ledger in `SKILL.md` step 2; signal breaks through scene texture           |
| **Moral language**      | Are cultures or institutions described with author judgment?                                               | Replace "evil/corrupt/civilized/barbaric" with inhabitant behavior                 |

## Runtime Scenario Tests

Use these as thought experiments before finalizing the world frame.

| Test                     | What to do                                                                              | What you want to see                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ordinary Street Test** | Ask what a random passerby notices in one ordinary location                             | Specific sensory detail tied to pressure, power, or system logic                               |
| **Unnamed NPC Test**     | Imagine a shopkeeper, guard, student, pilgrim, or clerk from this world                 | They behave differently from a generic genre NPC                                               |
| **Power Contact Test**   | Put an inhabitant in front of a tax collector, priest, guild officer, officer, or elder | Official story and actual mechanism both affect the interaction                                |
| **System Failure Test**  | Make magic/technology fail at a stressful moment                                        | The limit creates culture-specific tension, not arbitrary blockage                             |
| **Human Contact Test**   | Put a world rule into a tender, frightening, jealous, or intimate scene                 | The rule appears through feeling, posture, silence, taboo, or choice, not technical exposition |
| **Taboo Approach Test**  | Have someone almost mention a forbidden topic                                           | Avoidance behavior appears before exposition                                                   |
| **Anachronism Test**     | Mention medicine, law, science, identity, economy, or politics                          | The world's knowledge horizon filters the response                                             |
| **Default Drift Test**   | Strip proper nouns and reread the frame                                                 | The world still does not collapse into generic fantasy, cyberpunk, academy, or modern romance  |

## Anti-Pattern Checks

| Anti-pattern               | How it looks                                                                                         | Why it hurts                                 | Fix                                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wiki Summary**           | Lists geography, history, or institutions without scene effect                                       | The bot explains instead of inhabiting       | Convert facts into pressure and behavior                                      |
| **Single-Source Collapse** | One obvious culture, genre, or franchise reference dominates                                         | The model snaps to default imagery and norms | Add cross-source pressure or explicit Contrast                                |
| **Clean Power**            | Authority works exactly as advertised                                                                | Drama loses friction                         | Add official-vs-actual contradiction                                          |
| **Texture Vacuum**         | Systems are clever but ordinary life is blank                                                        | Scenes become generic prose                  | Add mundane sensory manifestations                                            |
| **Clinical Special Rules** | Magic, technology, class, or bonding rules describe feelings as data, processes, anatomy, or physics | People stop feeling like inhabitants         | Rewrite the rule through lived costs, social habits, touch, silence, and fear |
| **History Dump**           | Long timeline with little current consequence                                                        | The bot lectures                             | Keep only active history; move the rest to deep notes                         |
| **Omniscient Inhabitants** | NPCs know author-level lore or modern concepts                                                       | Immersion breaks                             | Add knowledge horizon limits                                                  |
| **Resolved Contradiction** | The draft hints that the dirty truth will soon be fixed                                              | RP loses active tension                      | Remove future resolution and keep the contradiction load-bearing              |
| **Moral Labeling**         | "Cruel empire," "corrupt city," "barbaric tribe"                                                     | Model softens or moralizes                   | Write what inhabitants do and tolerate                                        |
